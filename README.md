# BabyFund Thailand — Business Analyst Portfolio

**Tankhun Luengchaicharn | Junior IT Business Analyst case study**

BabyFund is a **simulated** Thai family savings and investment coordination concept. It explores how a guardian could plan long-term goals for a child, invite selected people to contribute on occasions such as birthdays, and follow each contribution through to a proposed licensed investment partner. The investment idea is S&P 500 exposure through a partner-approved product; BabyFund would not itself hold or invest customer money.

## What this portfolio demonstrates

- A problem hypothesis and business case, with competitor comparisons and explicit evidence limits.
- Traceable business requirements, functional requirements, user stories, process flows, use cases and proposed business rules.
- A reviewed clickable mockup captured in the wireframe document, plus test-case designs and calculation examples.
- Open decisions about payer identity, custody, legal ownership, fees, withdrawal and transfer at age 20.

## Case-study status

This is **not a launched product**. There is no real broker or payment partner, live investment account, customer-fund handling, executed UAT, legal approval or validated customer demand. The [Base44 mockup](https://baby-fund-bloom.base44.app/) is a design demonstration, not a working payment or investment service. Financial scenarios and revenue figures are stated assumptions, not forecasts or guaranteed returns.
